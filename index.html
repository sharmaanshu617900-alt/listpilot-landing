    Why it doesn't make things up

    Built so a wrong spec can't slip through

    The most common failure of AI listing tools is invented specifications — a wattage, material or size the product doesn't have. ListPilot's rules are designed against exactly that.

    Closed-world copy

    The writer may only use facts from your photo, supplier sheet and sentence. A separate checker lists every numeric or material claim in the draft and verifies each one against the input. Anything unverified becomes [NEEDED: …] and the listing is held.

    One product, one isolated run

    Each product is processed in a fresh context, so details from one product can never bleed into the next one in a batch of twenty.

    Draft first, approve to publish

    Nothing auto-publishes. You see the title, first image and price before anything goes live. Auto-publish becomes an option only after 10 approved listings with zero corrections — per store, opt-in.

    A hard no-claims list

    No “eco-friendly”, health, certification, award or “best” language — and no invented compatibility (“fits iPhone…”) — unless the exact wording is in your input.

    FAQ

    Is ListPilot live yet?+

    It's in a 30-day validation test. During the test, listings are produced with the agent workflow and human-checked before publishing — the same 8-point checklist, the same approve step. If the test passes its bar (sellers approving listings as-is, and coming back with a second batch), the Shopify app gets built next. If it doesn't pass, it doesn't get built — that's the point of testing first.

    Will it invent product details?+

    No — that's the core rule. Copy is written only from your photo, supplier sheet and sentence. A checker verifies every number, material and claim against that input; anything it can't verify is flagged [NEEDED: …] and the listing is held until you supply it.

    Do I approve before anything goes live?+

    Always. Listings are created as drafts in your store; you see the title, first image and price and tap Approve. Nothing publishes unseen.

    What exactly do I send?+

    One or more product photos, plus a supplier link or CSV row if you have one, and the price if you know it. One sentence is enough for the goal — the sample sentence in the form above is the real one sellers use.

    Which platforms does it work with?+

    Shopify only, deliberately — one store platform, one taxonomy, one publishing flow, done properly. Amazon, eBay and Etsy have different rules and are a separate job.

    Why not just use Shopify Magic? It's free.+

    Magic is a good writer for one field at a time — but you still prompt it field by field, paste the results together, check the facts yourself and publish. ListPilot's job starts where Magic stops: photo in, finished and checked listing live, with a receipt.
